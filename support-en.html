FAQ

How do I add people and share my location? In Friends, send an invite via code, QR code or link, or request by phone number; the other person accepts after signing in. Then each of you enables sharing in Profile → Location & Notifications and grants system location permission to see each other's location.

How do I stop sharing my location? Turn off sharing in Profile → Location & Notifications to stop collection and sharing. You can also remove a friend or leave a circle.

How do I set arrive/leave alerts? In Profile → Saved places, add Home, Work or a custom place and set the alert radius. You will be notified when someone arrives at or leaves it.

How do I set low-battery alerts? You are notified by default when a device battery drops below 20%; you can manage the switch in notification settings.

How does One-tap SOS work? Does it replace emergency calls? SOS sends a prominent alert to members of your circles along with your ongoing location, and they can contact 110 for you. It does not replace official emergency services; in an emergency, always call 110 directly.

How long is location kept? Location and battery data are kept for the most recent 7 days and auto-cleaned.

How do I delete data or my account? You can delete location and notification data in the App; to delete your account and all data, contact us.
