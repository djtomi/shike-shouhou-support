Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

1. Introduction

时刻守候 ("the App") is a private real-time location guardian app for family, couples, close friends and friends, operated by tomi ("we"). We understand how sensitive location data is. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use, share and protect it, and the rights you have. By using the App you accept this policy.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account: the phone number and password you provide when registering or signing in, used to create your account, sign in and maintain relationships.
  • Location: collected only after you explicitly enable location sharing and grant system location permission. This includes real-time location and daily trail (dwell points, movement mode, distance, etc.). Coordinates are normalized to WGS-84 and only reported when the system accuracy error is within 50 m. Location data is kept for the most recent 7 days and auto-cleaned afterwards.
  • Battery: your phone battery level reported with valid location samples, used for low-battery alerts and the 24-hour battery curve.
  • Device: device model and the name of the connected Wi-Fi (attached to location samples only while sharing is on, for detail display; we do not collect Wi-Fi passwords or BSSID).
  • Status: the work / rest / moving / etc. status you set, with its validity period.
  • Avatar: the avatar thumbnail you upload (stored after metadata removal).
  • Relationships and circles: the circles, groups and friend relationships you create.
  • Notification token: used to deliver arrive/leave, low-battery, status and SOS messages to you via Apple APNs.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information for location sharing, daily trail replay, arrive/leave and low-battery alerts, battery history, status sync, SOS messaging, account and relationship management, and security and troubleshooting. We do not use your information for ad targeting, profiling, or selling it to third parties.

4. How We Share and Disclose

Sharing with your people: your location and trail are visible only to members you have explicitly authorized and who join the same circle. Sharing is mutual and revocable; you can revoke it or leave a circle at any time, and collection and sharing stop once revoked.

Third-party services: maps and location are provided by the AMap SDK (Beijing AutoNavi Co., Ltd.). Once you consent and enable it on first use, AMap processes the location, device and network information needed for map display and positioning under its own privacy policy (AMap Privacy Policy). Messages are delivered through Apple APNs; the device token is used only for this App's notifications. Data is stored on Tencent CloudBase (Shanghai, China) and transmitted over HTTPS. There are currently no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Legal disclosure: we may disclose information as required by law, by regulators or courts, or to protect your or others' legitimate rights and interests.

5. Your Choices and Control

  • Location sharing switch: enable or disable sharing anytime in Profile → Location & Notifications. Turning it off stops collection and sharing.
  • System permissions: manage this App's location (while using / always), notifications, camera and photo permissions in system settings.
  • Notification switches: turn arrive/leave and low-battery alerts on or off individually.
  • Data deletion: delete location and notification data tied to your account; to delete your entire account and all data, contact us via the support page.
  • Remove friends / leave circles: remove a friend or leave a circle anytime to revoke the corresponding sharing permission.

6. Data Retention

Location and battery data are kept for the most recent 7 days and auto-cleaned; sessions last up to 30 days; invitation links are valid for 24 hours and can be used once.

7. Minors

This App is primarily intended for adults. When guardian features are used for a minor, we recommend a parent or guardian manage the account and sharing. We do not intentionally collect personal information from children under 13; if we learn we have collected it, we will delete it promptly.

8. Data Security

We protect your data with HTTPS transmission, encrypted storage, mutual-authorization checks, access control and scheduled cleanup. No internet transmission or storage can be guaranteed absolutely secure, so please keep your password safe.

9. One-Tap SOS

One-tap SOS sends a prominent alert to members of your circles along with your ongoing real-time location, and suggests they contact emergency services (110). The App does not replace or guarantee official emergency response; in an emergency, always call 110 or your local emergency number first.

10. Policy Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page and in the App, and material changes will be notified. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
